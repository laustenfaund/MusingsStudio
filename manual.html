User manual

Musings studio

Notes and picture-card decks for “Come, Follow Me” Musings. It runs in your browser, works offline, and can keep a copy in your Google Drive.

01

Getting around

Musings Studio is for writing down thoughts for "Come, Follow Me" Musings and turning them into picture cards to post.

The bar at the bottom has three places:

  • Notes for jotting things down.
  • Decks for your sets of cards. A deck is one post.
  • Library for Google Drive, fonts, saved designs and backups.

Everything saves by itself as you go. There's no save button to remember.

The app starts with an example note, an example deck, and a template called "Musings, 8 cards", so you can see how things look. Change or delete them whenever you like.

02

Notes

Notes are for anything you want to keep: a verse, a line, a song, a photo of a notepad page.

  1. Type in the box at the top.
  2. To add a picture, tap Add photo, or paste a picture into the box.
  3. Tap Save note. On a computer, Ctrl+Enter (or Cmd+Enter on a Mac) also saves.

From Drive works a little differently: each photo you pick from your Google Drive is saved straight away as its own note. Dropping photos onto the app does the same.

Your notes appear below, newest first. Use the search box to find one by its words.

Each note has three buttons:

  • Edit to change its words.
  • Send to deck to turn it into a new card. Pick a deck, or New deck. The note's words become text on the card and its photo becomes the background.
  • Delete. This can't be undone.

03

Decks

On the Decks page:

  • New deck makes a deck with 5 empty cards, named after the week (for example "Week of Sep 21").
  • Start from… makes a deck from a saved template, or from a copy of a recent deck. Copies keep the text and layout but not the photos.

Each deck shows its first card. Tap it, or Open, to work on it. Rename changes its name. More has:

  • Duplicate: a full copy, photos included.
  • Save as template: keeps its cards and text styles to start future decks from.
  • Delete deck: removes it for good.

04

Inside a deck

An open deck shows all its cards. From here you can:

  • Tap a card to edit it.
  • Use + to add an empty card, or − to remove the last card.
  • Change the card size: 4:5 (Facebook), 3:4, 1:1 (square) or 9:16 (tall, for stories).
  • Drag cards to change their order. On a phone, press and hold a card first.
  • Drop a photo onto a card to make it that card's background.
  • Type in the name box at the top to rename the deck.

Export turns the cards into pictures. See "Sharing your cards" below.

05

Editing a card

The card is shown large. Tap a piece of text on it to select it, then drag to move it. It snaps to the middle when you get close.

Use ‹ and › at the top to go to the previous or next card, and ‹ All cards to go back to the whole deck.

The buttons under the card:

  • Move earlier and Move later change the card's place in the deck.
  • Use card type swaps in a saved design. It replaces the card's text, but keeps your photo.
  • Duplicate copies the card.
  • Save as card type saves this card's design to reuse. See "Card types and templates".
  • Delete card.

Beside the card are three tabs: Text, Background and Notes.

Text

The buttons across the top list each piece of text on the card. Tap one to select it, or + Text to add a new one. New text starts in the last style you used.

For the selected text you can change the words, font, size, weight, italic, color, alignment, width, line spacing, see-through amount (opacity) and shadow. You can also:

  • Put a box behind it, around each line or the whole block. Pick its shape (square, rounded or pill), color and see-through amount.
  • Center it across or up and down.
  • Bring forward to put it on top of other text.
  • Duplicate or delete it.

Background

  • Add photo, From Drive, or Photo from notes to use a photo from one of your notes.
  • Zoom, and slide the photo left, right, up or down to frame it.
  • Tone: None, Sepia, Warm, or Black & white, with a strength slider.
  • Darken, to make the text easier to read.
  • With no photo, the card is a fade between a top color and a bottom color that you choose.

Notes

Shows your notes, with a search box. Drag a note onto the card, or use its buttons. Add text adds its words to the card. Use photo makes its photo the background.

06

Card types and templates

  • A card type is the design of one card: its text pieces, fonts and colors. The app comes with Title, Statement, Whisper, Quote + credit, Scripture and Response. Save your own with Save as card type, and use it on any card with Use card type.
  • A template is a whole deck's design, without photos. Save one from the Decks page with More, then Save as template. Start a new deck from it with Start from….

In Library you can rename or delete card types and templates. Templates also have Use, which starts a new deck from them.

07

Fonts

The app comes with 11 fonts. The ones you use most are listed first.

In Library, under Fonts:

  • Add Google font: type the font's name exactly as it appears on fonts.google.com.
  • Upload font file: a .ttf, .otf, .woff or .woff2 file. You can also drop a font file anywhere on the app.
  • Font from Drive: pick a font file from your Google Drive.
  • Remove takes a font off the list. Cards already using it keep their words, but may show in a stand-in font.

08

Sharing your cards

Open a deck and tap Export. The app makes a full-size picture of every card (1080 pixels wide). Then:

  • Share… opens your phone's share sheet, so you can send the cards straight to Facebook.
  • Save to Drive puts them in your Drive, in Musings Studio › Decks › the deck's name. This needs Drive connected.
  • Save all (.zip) downloads them as one zip file.
  • Save under a picture downloads just that one. On a phone you can also press and hold a picture to save it to your photos.

09

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive keeps a copy of everything in your Drive, and lets you use the app on more than one device.

  1. Go to Library and tap Connect Google Drive.
  2. Sign in with your Google account. If Google says it "hasn't verified this app", tap Continue. That's normal for a private app.

After that, the app syncs on its own a few seconds after you change something, and whenever you open it. The button at the top right shows how it's going: Syncing…, Synced, Offline, Tap to sync, or Sync problem. Tap it if it says Tap to sync or Sync problem.

If you change things on two devices before they've synced, the app asks which version to keep: the one in Drive, or the one on this device.

Your Drive gets one folder:

Musings Studio/
  musings-data.json    notes, decks, card types, templates, fonts list
  Notes.txt            your notes as plain text
  Photos and fonts/    every photo and font the app uses
  Decks/               pictures saved with Save to Drive

The app can only see files it made and files you pick. It can't see the rest of your Drive.

Disconnect stops syncing on this device. Your files stay in Drive.

10

Backups

Without Drive, everything lives only in this browser on this device. If the browser's data is cleared, it's gone. So now and then, go to Library and tap Save backup file. That saves one zip file with all your notes, decks, photos and fonts.

Restore from backup loads a backup file. It replaces everything in the app with what's in the file.

The bottom of the Library page shows how much space the app is using.

11

On your phone

Put the app on your home screen so it opens like any other app:

  • iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap Install app, or use the menu's Add to Home screen. Library may also show an Install app button.

It works without internet. Drive sync, Google fonts and sharing need a connection. When there's an update, the app picks it up the next time it's opened with internet.

12

Troubleshooting

"Couldn't save. Your browser storage may be full.": save a backup file, then delete decks or notes with photos you no longer need.

"Not saving" at the top: this browser isn't letting the app save, which often happens in private browsing. Use a normal window, or the home-screen app.

"Connect Google Drive first, in Library.": Save to Drive and From Drive need Drive connected.

Sync problem: tap the button at the top right to reconnect.

A Google font can't be found: check the spelling against fonts.google.com.

"The zip tool didn't load": reload the app with internet, or save the pictures one at a time.
